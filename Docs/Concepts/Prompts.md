# Concept Generation Briefs

These concept frames were created with the built-in ImageGen workflow as visual-development references. They are not final game assets.

All three briefs share these constraints:

- original stylized 3D;
- wide 16:9 gameplay composition;
- readable bakery production chain;
- four distinct baked-product silhouettes;
- flour cream, bread crust, cocoa, sage, sour cherry, oven glow, and evening blue palette;
- no borrowed branding, logos, watermarks, floating rewards, or generic mobile-game chrome.

## 01 — Soft Sculpted Afternoon

```text
Create an original, irresistibly charming open-side bakery food truck staged as a tiny living diorama at late afternoon. Show the full truck interior in one elevated three-quarter view: refrigerator, warm oven, compact baker, service counter, and one elderly customer. Display Country Bread, Kaiser Rolls, a Butter Croissant, and a Cinnamon Swirl with clear gameplay-scale silhouettes. Use softly sculpted miniature geometry, broad clean forms, subtle hand-finished wear, warm oven practical light, cool ambient separation, and a restrained wooden bottom recipe rail with four picture-only paper cards. Avoid photorealism, generic glossy mobile UI, excessive bloom, clutter, anime styling, black outlines, text, logos, and reward effects.
```

## 02 — Hand-Painted Market Morning

```text
Create an original open-side bakery food truck on an unmistakably tiny rounded street diorama in a hand-painted market-morning direction. Keep the entire diorama visible. Show a refrigerator, one oven, a compact stylized baker, a friendly grandmother, and trays of Country Bread, Kaiser Rolls, Croissants, and Cinnamon Swirls. Use simplified geometric forms, matte hand-painted materials, graphic proportions, bright soft morning light, strong silhouettes, and a shallow wooden recipe rack with four image-only cards. Avoid cinematic photorealism, plastic toy surfaces, generic buttons, gradients, dense decoration, text, logos, and reward effects.
```

## 03 — Tactile Evening Bakery

```text
Create the upgraded level-two state of the same tiny bakery: a small open-front wooden bakery theatre on a rounded evening street diorama. Show the full chain with refrigerator, two ovens, baker, manager with clipboard, glazing bench, counter, grandmother, and one younger customer. Keep all four products visually distinct. Use tactile storybook miniature geometry, hand-finished wood and painted metal, compact characters, blue-hour ambient light, restrained warm practicals, a picture-only recipe rail, and a closed ledger at the edge. Render the sign "Baka-Bake-Bakery" exactly once above the opening. Avoid giant architecture, photorealism, generic mobile UI, purple neon, excessive bloom, clutter, text beyond the sign, logos, and reward effects.
```
