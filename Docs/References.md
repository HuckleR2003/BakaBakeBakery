# Reference Study

References are used to identify design principles, not to reproduce another game's identity, assets, interface, or composition.

## Visual and interaction references

### Minami Lane

<https://store.steampowered.com/app/2678990/Minami_Lane/>

- A complete management space remains readable as one small living scene.
- Warm environmental rhythm can carry hierarchy without heavy interface framing.
- Buildings and inhabitants communicate progress together.

### Lemon Cake

<https://store.steampowered.com/app/1338330/Lemon_Cake/>

- Workstations are spatially legible.
- Bakery repair and expansion create visible progression.
- Product handling benefits from broad, readable character motion.

### Good Pizza, Great Pizza

<https://mail.goodpizzagreatpizza.com/press/>

- Customer requests can carry personality and function simultaneously.
- Food-service UI feels strongest when it borrows from tickets, counters, and printed menus.
- Repetition gains texture through a varied customer cast.

### Cats & Soup

<https://www.catsnsoup.com/>

- Idle production remains entertaining because every automated step is animated.
- Strong silhouettes let many small actions remain readable.
- Calm pacing does not require an inactive world.

### Tiny Bookshop

<https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/tiny-bookshop/>

- A mobile shop can still feel personal and spatially rich.
- Customisation works when placed items retain physical presence and meaning.
- Soft environmental storytelling supports a small management loop.

### Camper Van: Make it Home

<https://campervangame.com/>

- Compact interiors benefit from tactile placement and restrained camera framing.
- Material contrast can create charm without dense surface detail.
- A miniature space feels believable when every object has a clear home.

## Internal synthesis

Baka Bake Bakery combines none of these games directly. Its own target is:

> A softly sculpted bakery theatre where manual work gradually becomes a tiny, visible machine.

The food truck cutaway, four-command tutorial, cursor-driven stage reveal, physical recipe rail, and truck-to-neon-bakery transformation form the project's distinct centre.

## Product identity checks

- La Lorraine Bakery Group describes monokle as semi-laminated pastries formed into a spiral and filled generously: <https://llbg.com/pl-PL/kategorie/monokle>
- Its cinnamon version is a Danish-style yeast pastry with a cinnamon and sugar filling: <https://llbg.com/pl-PL/produkty/5002071-monokl-z-nadzieniem-cynamonowym>
- Finezja follows the project's supplied product brief: a soft pastry with visibly interwoven white vanilla and strawberry creams. It is treated as an authored Baka Bake Bakery recipe rather than a claim about one standardized market product.
